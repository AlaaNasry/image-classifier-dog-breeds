Questions regarding Uploaded Image Classification:

1. Did the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: No. VGG and ResNet both classified Dog_01.jpg as a beagle. AlexNet classified Dog_01.jpg as a basset/basset hound.


2. Did each of the three model architectures classify the breed of dog in Dog_01.jpg to be the same breed of dog as that model architecture classified Dog_02.jpg? If not, report the differences in the classifications.

Answer: VGG and ResNet classified both Dog_01.jpg and Dog_02.jpg as a beagle. AlexNet did not: it classified Dog_01.jpg as a basset/basset hound and Dog_02.jpg as a beagle.


3. Did the three model architectures correctly classify Animal_Name_01.jpg and Object_Name_01.jpg to not be dogs? If not, report the misclassifications.

Answer: Yes. All three model architectures correctly classified Cat_01.jpg as not a dog, and all three correctly classified Galiwa_(apple).jpg as not a dog. VGG and ResNet identified the apple image as a granny smith, while AlexNet identified it as a pomegranate; neither classification was a dog.


4. Based upon your answers for questions 1. - 3. above, select the model architecture that you feel did the best at classifying the four uploaded images. Describe why you selected that model architecture as the best on uploaded image classification.

Answer: VGG is the best choice for these four uploaded images, although ResNet performed equally well on this particular set. VGG and ResNet both classified Dog_01.jpg and Dog_02.jpg as beagles and correctly classified both the cat and apple images as not dogs. AlexNet was less consistent because it classified Dog_01.jpg as a basset/basset hound but Dog_02.jpg as a beagle. VGG is selected as the best because it was fully consistent on the two dog images and correctly rejected both non-dog images; it also performed best overall on the 40-image project dataset.
